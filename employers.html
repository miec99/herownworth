What we ask of you

Four things. That's all.

  • A room

    Anywhere the group can sit together with a table. A staff canteen or training room is ideal.

  • Paid time

    About four hours in total. Sessions on unpaid time have poor attendance, and attendance is the whole point.

  • A list

    The names of women who would like to take part. Participation is always voluntary.

  • A contact

    One person on your side who can confirm dates and remind the group.

What it costs

A flat fee per participant.

The fee covers the teaching, printed materials in the participants' language and the three-month follow-up. Participants pay nothing, and nothing is ever sold to them. Write to us with the size of your team and we will send a quote.

For small hosts and non-profits we will try to find a way. Ask.

Why hosts do it

Money trouble follows people to work.

Staff who understand their own budgets borrow less from employers and moneylenders, miss fewer days and stay longer. Hosts also tell us it is simply the right thing to do for the women who keep their businesses running.

Our first host was a beach resort in Alibag, where 20 women completed the course in 2026. Its name will appear here with permission.
