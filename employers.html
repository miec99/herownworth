For employers

Host the programme for the women on your staff.

You provide a room and five short sessions on paid time. We provide the teaching, the materials and the follow-up. Your staff leave with a budget of their own and a clearer head about money.

Five sessions of 45 minutesone a week, or two a week over three weeks
Any group from 8 to 25 womenlarger teams run as two groups
Marathi, Hindi or Englishmaterials in the participants' language

What we ask of you

  • A room

    Anywhere the group can sit together with a table. A staff canteen or training room is ideal.

  • Paid time

    About four hours in total over the course. Sessions on unpaid time have poor attendance, and attendance is the whole point.

  • A list

    The names of women who would like to take part. Participation is always voluntary.

  • A contact

    One person on your side who can confirm dates and remind the group.

What it costs

Employers pay a flat fee per participant for the course, which covers the teaching, printed materials in the participants' language and the three-month follow-up. Participants pay nothing, and nothing is ever sold to them. Write to us with the size of your team and we will send a quote.

For small hosts and non-profits we will try to find a way. Ask.

Why hosts do it

Money trouble follows people to work. Staff who understand their own budgets borrow less from employers and moneylenders, miss fewer days, and stay longer. Hosts also tell us it is simply the right thing to do for the women who keep their businesses running.

Our first host was a beach resort in Alibag, where 20 women completed the course in 2026. Their names and the resort's will appear here with permission.

What we are and are not. Her Own Worth is a financial education programme. It does not give investment advice, does not recommend, sell or distribute any financial product, and is not registered as an investment adviser. Sessions explain how money, saving, insurance and borrowing work in plain language; every decision stays with the participant.
