How it began

Two things, seen side by side.

Founded by Sanaa Jethani, who has worked in finance since 2021.

The idea came from two things seen together: how much wealth sat with a very few people she had studied alongside, and how little financial knowledge had reached the women who cooked, cleaned and ran the front desks of the places she visited. A friend's family resort in Alibag agreed to host the first course. Twenty women on its staff took part.

The work has not stayed in Alibag. Sanaa has also run money seminars for staff at clubs in Mumbai, and the same questions come up in every room.

What the first cohort taught us shaped everything on this site: that the account is rarely hers, that borrowing is the most urgent question, and that many of these women have saved carefully for decades without anyone explaining how to make that saving work harder.

Our mission

One standard of financial education. Whatever she earns.

A woman earning Rs 15,000 a month deserves the same clear, patient explanation of money that a wealthy family gets from its adviser. That is the standard we teach to, and we believe it is how it should be everywhere.

What we believe

Four rules we don't bend.

  • The same standard for everyone

    The quality of explanation a wealthy client gets should not depend on income. Same material, same patience, same respect, in every room we teach in.

  • Education, not advice

    We explain how money works. We do not tell anyone what to buy, and we do not sell anything. Every decision belongs to the participant.

  • Her own name

    Independence means an account, a budget and a plan that are hers, whatever her household looks like.

  • Measure honestly

    We report what we observe, in aggregate and with consent, and we say plainly what we do not know.
