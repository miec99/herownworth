About

It started with twenty women and a beach resort.

How it began

Her Own Worth was founded in Mumbai in 2026 by Sanaa Jethani, who has worked in finance since 2021.

The idea came from two things seen side by side: how much wealth sat with a very few people she had studied alongside, and how little financial knowledge had reached the women who cooked, cleaned and ran the front desks of the places she visited. A friend's family resort in Alibag agreed to host the first course. Twenty women on its staff took part.

What the first cohort taught us shaped everything on this site: that the account is rarely hers, that borrowing is the most urgent question, and that many of these women have saved carefully for decades without anyone ever explaining to them how to make that saving work harder.

What we believe

  • Education, not advice

    We explain how money works. We do not tell anyone what to buy, and we do not sell anything. Every decision belongs to the participant.

  • Her own name

    Independence means an account, a budget and a plan that are hers, whatever her household looks like.

  • The same standard for everyone

    The quality of explanation a wealthy client gets should not depend on income. We teach to that standard.

  • Measure honestly

    We report what we observe, in aggregate and with consent, and we say plainly what we do not know.
