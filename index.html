Money in her own name.

Her Own Worth teaches women earning between Rs 10,000 and Rs 30,000 a month how money works: budgeting, saving, insurance, borrowing and planning for old age. Sessions happen where they work, in their own language, on paid time.

Why it exists

India's wealth is unusually concentrated. The World Inequality Lab estimates that the richest 1% hold about 40% of national wealth. At the other end, women in low-paid work are the least likely of any group to have savings, insurance or a pension in their own name.

The gap is not only money. It is information. Nobody has explained compounding, or what an emergency fund is for, or how to compare a moneylender's rate with a bank's. Her Own Worth exists to close that gap, one workplace at a time.

How it works

  • The employer hosts

    A room, five short sessions on paid time, and a list of the women who want to take part. Nothing else is asked of the employer.

  • We teach

    Five sessions of about 45 minutes, in Marathi or Hindi, built around the participants' own monthly budgets. Plain language, no jargon, no products.

  • She decides

    Each woman leaves with a written budget she made herself, a list of the questions to ask any bank or agent, and the confidence to act on her own account.

  • We check back

    A short, voluntary survey before the programme and three months after it tells us what changed and what we should teach better. Results are reported only in anonymised form.

Have women on your payroll?

Five sessions, one room, paid time. We bring everything else. Resorts, hotels, restaurants, factories, housing societies and households with domestic staff are all good hosts.

How to host the programme
What we are and are not. Her Own Worth is a financial education programme. It does not give investment advice, does not recommend, sell or distribute any financial product, and is not registered as an investment adviser. Sessions explain how money, saving, insurance and borrowing work in plain language; every decision stays with the participant.
