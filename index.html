Why we exist

40%

Of India's wealth sits with one percent of Indians.

At the other end, women in low-paid work are the least likely of any group to hold savings, insurance or a pension in their own name.

The gap is not only money. It is information. Nobody has ever explained compounding to them, or what an emergency fund is for, or how to compare a moneylender's rate with a bank's. Her Own Worth closes that gap, one workplace at a time.

Wealth share from the World Inequality Lab, 2022-23.

How it works

Five sessions. One room. Her own budget.

1

The employer hosts

A room, five short sessions on paid time, and a list of women who want to take part. Nothing else is asked.

2

We teach

Five sessions of about 45 minutes in Marathi or Hindi, built around each participant's own monthly budget. No jargon, no products.

3

She decides

Each woman leaves with a budget in her own hand, the questions to ask any bank or agent, and the confidence to act on her own account.

4

We check back

A short, voluntary survey before and three months after tells us what changed. Results are reported only in anonymised form.

Our standard

The explanation a wealthy client gets should not depend on income.

We teach to that standard. Every participant gets the same clarity, the same patience and the same respect, whatever she earns.
